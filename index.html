Research Areas

Datacenter

Advanced power electronics for high-efficiency, high-density, and reliable data center energy systems.

Titanium 1U PSULiquid coolingBridgeless PFC

Innovative power conversion and charging technologies for efficient and sustainable electric vehicles.

Aerospace

Lightweight and high-reliability power electronics for aerospace propulsion and onboard energy systems.
